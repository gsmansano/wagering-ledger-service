## Decisions and notes

- ORM: MikroORM (decision #1). Preferred by the specification; `em.transactional()` and `LockMode` fit the single-transaction write path. A forked `EntityManager` per request or message is required to avoid stale Identity Map reads.
- SQS emulator: MiniStack (decision #2). Runs without an account token, so a clean clone starts with `docker compose up`. FIFO and DLQ behaviour must be verified in M1; LocalStack is the fallback.
- Language: English for code, documentation and commits (decision #3).
- Bun's installer adds its `PATH` entry to `~/.bashrc`, which non-interactive shells do not read. Use `~/.bun/bin/bun` or add the entry to `~/.profile`.
